FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on the service:

  • IMEI Unlocks & Credits: 30 seconds to 5 minutes (typically under 90 seconds)
  • Tool Activations: Instant to 15 minutes
  • MDM / iCloud Bypass: 5 to 60 minutes
  • Gift Cards & Digital Goods: Instant

You'll receive an email and dashboard notification the moment your order completes.

My order is stuck on "Processing" — what should I do?

Most orders complete within 5 minutes. If your order has been on "Processing" for over 30 minutes:

  • Refresh your My Orders page
  • Check your email spam folder for delivery notification
  • Some services (especially MDM / iCloud) can take up to 24 hours during peak demand
  • If it exceeds the estimated time, contact us with your order reference

We monitor every order automatically and will refund you if the service provider fails to deliver.

What is your refund policy?

We offer automatic refunds in these cases:

  • The service provider rejects your order
  • The unlock code cannot be found for your device
  • Delivery fails within 24 hours of payment

Refunds are credited to your wallet balance instantly, or returned to your original payment method within 3–5 business days if requested.

Note: Once a successful unlock code is delivered and you've submitted it to the device, we cannot refund — even if the code doesn't work, unless the provider verifies a fault.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:

  • Mobile Money: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa, MTN MoMo
  • Crypto: Bitcoin, USDT (TRC20/ERC20), Ethereum, Binance Pay
  • Bank Transfer: Local and international (SWIFT)
  • Card: Visa, Mastercard (via Stripe)
  • PayPal: Select regions

All payments are encrypted with SSL and processed through PCI-DSS compliant gateways.

How do I top up my wallet?

Go to Wallet → Top Up, choose your payment method, and enter the amount. Your balance updates instantly for crypto and mobile money, or within 1–2 hours for bank transfers.

Wallet balance never expires and can be used across all services.

Do you offer bulk / reseller pricing?

Yes. We offer tiered wholesale pricing for high-volume customers:

  • Silver: $500+ monthly spend → 5% discount
  • Gold: $2,000+ monthly spend → 10% discount
  • Platinum: $5,000+ monthly spend → 15% discount + priority support

Contact us with your business details and expected monthly volume to apply.

Is my personal data safe?

Yes. We take security seriously:

  • All traffic is encrypted with TLS 1.3
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • Payment data is never stored on our servers
  • IMEI and device data are used only for order fulfillment and deleted after 90 days

See our full Privacy Policy for details.

Do you support legacy tools and old devices?

Absolutely. We support devices from as far back as 2005 — including legacy Nokia, BlackBerry, and early Android devices. Our server network includes Chimera, Hydra, Infinity, ATF, and legacy-specific tools.

If a tool you need isn't listed, message us — we can usually source it within 24 hours.
