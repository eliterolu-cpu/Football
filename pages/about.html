Our Story

From a small repair shop to a global platform

GSM TECH HUB started in 2019 as a small mobile repair shop in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Like most technicians, we struggled with inconsistent unlock suppliers, slow delivery, and unclear pricing.

We decided to build something better — a platform that combines direct supplier access, wholesale pricing, and automated fulfillment so technicians can focus on their customers instead of chasing codes.

Today, we serve over 12,400 resellers and repair shops across 195 countries. Every order runs through our automated API connection to GSM Africa and other tier-1 suppliers, ensuring the fastest possible delivery at the best possible price.

We're not just a reseller — we're technicians who understand the tools, the devices, and the urgency of your customers walking out the door.
